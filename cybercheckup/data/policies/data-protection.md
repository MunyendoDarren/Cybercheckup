## Rules
1. **Collect only what we need**, and tell people why we need it. Put a short privacy notice at the till or on forms.
2. **Keep it out of sight.** Paper records are locked away. Screens face away from customers. Do not post customer details in group chats.
3. **Restrict access.** Sensitive records are available only to those who need them for their job.
4. **Keep it safe.** Devices are locked and updated, and files are backed up.
5. **Keep it only as long as needed**, then delete or destroy it securely.
6. **Respect people's rights.** If someone asks what we hold about them, or asks us to correct or delete it, tell {{owner}} the same day.
7. **Report a leak at once.** If personal data may have been lost, stolen or sent to the wrong person, follow the incident response plan. Check your regulator's current guidance, as some laws require reporting serious breaches within a short time (in Kenya, to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner, ODPC).
