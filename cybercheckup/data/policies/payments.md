# Payment verification policy

**Business:** {{business_name}}  
**Policy owner:** {{owner}}

## Why we have this policy
Payment fraud is the fastest way a small business loses real money. Criminals copy invoices, pretend to be suppliers or bosses, and change account details.

## Rules
1. **Call back before paying new details.** If a supplier, landlord, school, funder or manager sends new bank, paybill or till details by message or email, confirm by phone using a number we already had. Do not use the number in the message.
2. **Two people for large payments.** Payments above the limit below, and any change to payment details, need a second person's approval.
3. **Urgency is a warning sign.** Pressure to pay today and keep it secret is how scams work. Stop and check.
4. **Check the money has arrived.** For mobile money or bank payments received, check the balance in the official app or statement. A screenshot or message is not proof.
5. **Protect the money phones.** Phones and SIMs used for mobile money have a SIM PIN, a screen lock and a wallet PIN known only to the account holder. PINs are never written down or shared.
6. **Review statements weekly.** Someone checks bank and mobile money statements every week and reports anything unfamiliar the same day.
7. **If money is sent to the wrong place,** call the bank or mobile money provider immediately and follow the incident response plan.
