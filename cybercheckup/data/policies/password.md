# Passwords and account access policy

**Business:** {{business_name}}  
**Policy owner:** {{owner}}  
**Applies to:** everyone who uses a business account, phone or computer ({{staff_count}} people).

## Why we have this policy
Stolen and guessed logins are the most common way small businesses are broken into. These rules close that door.

## Rules
1. **One password per account.** Never reuse a password. Use the business password manager to create and store them.
2. **Make them long.** Use a passphrase of 12 characters or more, or let the password manager generate one.
3. **Two-step verification is on** for business email, banking, mobile money, social media and accounting software. Use an authenticator app where possible.
4. **One login per person.** Do not share your login or ask anyone to share theirs. Shared pages get admin roles, not shared passwords.
5. **Never write passwords or PINs** on sticky notes, on the phone, or in a notebook kept with the device.
6. **Change a password immediately** if you suspect it was seen, typed into a suspicious page, or reused on a site that was breached. Tell {{owner}}.
7. **Recovery.** Each key account has a second admin and a recovery email that the business controls.
