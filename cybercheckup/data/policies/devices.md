# Devices, Wi-Fi and mobile phone policy

**Business:** {{business_name}}  
**Policy owner:** {{owner}}

## Why we have this policy
Phones, computers and the Wi-Fi router hold our money, records and customer details. Keeping them updated, locked and tracked prevents most common attacks.

## Devices
1. **Keep a list** of business computers, phones, tablets and other devices: who uses each, where it is kept, and when it was bought.
2. **Updates are automatic.** Computers and phones install updates automatically and are restarted weekly.
3. **Antivirus is on** (Windows Security is fine) and up to date.
4. **Screens lock automatically** within two minutes and need a PIN of 6+ digits, a password or a fingerprint.
5. **Lost or stolen devices.** Find My Device is switched on so devices can be locked or wiped. Report a loss to {{owner}} immediately.
6. **Licensed or open-source software only.** No cracked or pirated programs.
7. **Card machines and POS terminals** are checked each morning for tampering and locked away overnight.

## Wi-Fi and network
1. The router's **admin password is changed** from the default, and remote administration is off.
2. Business Wi-Fi uses **WPA2 or WPA3** with a password of 12+ characters. It is changed when someone who knew it leaves.
3. **Customers use a separate guest Wi-Fi.**
4. **Cameras, printers and smart devices** have changed passwords, current firmware and no unneeded remote access.
