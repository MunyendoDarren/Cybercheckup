## Why we have this policy
Ransomware, a failed disk, theft or a fire can destroy our records overnight. Backups let us carry on.

## Rules
1. **Back up automatically** at least weekly, and daily for records that change every day.
2. **Keep three copies, in two places, with one away from the business.** For example: the working copy, one cloud copy, and one drive that is unplugged and kept somewhere else.
3. **One copy must be out of reach of ransomware.** A drive that is always plugged in does not count.
4. **Protect backups** with a strong password and two-step verification on the cloud account.
5. **Test a restore every six months.** Restore two files and record the date and result below.
6. **Staff save work in the shared, backed-up folder**, never only on a desktop or phone.
