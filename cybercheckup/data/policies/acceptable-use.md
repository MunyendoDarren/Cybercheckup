# Staff acceptable use and security rules

**Business:** {{business_name}}  
**Policy owner:** {{owner}}

## Why we have this policy
Everyone who handles money, customer details or business accounts is part of our security. These rules are short on purpose.

## Our rules
1. **Think before you click.** Messages that are urgent, too good to be true, or ask for money, codes or logins are suspicious until checked.
2. **Never share a PIN or one-time code**, even with someone who says they are from the bank, the telecom or the boss. Hang up and call back on the official number.
3. **Use business accounts for business.** Customer details and business files stay in business systems, not personal email, personal cloud accounts or group chats.
4. **Keep your devices locked** and up to date. Report a lost or stolen device to {{owner}} immediately.
5. **Only install approved software.** No cracked or pirated programs.
6. **Report fast.** If you clicked something, paid something or lost something, tell {{owner}} straight away. Reporting quickly is never punished.
7. **Access matches the job.** You get the access your role needs. Do not ask others to lend you theirs.

## Joiners, movers and leavers
- **When someone joins:** create their own logins, give the access their role needs, and run the staff briefing.
- **When someone changes role:** remove access they no longer need.
- **When someone leaves (same day):** disable their accounts, remove them from shared pages and groups, change shared passwords and the Wi-Fi password if they knew it, and collect keys and devices.

## Outside helpers
Web designers, accountants, IT helpers and freelancers get their own logins, limited to the job. Remove access when the job ends.
