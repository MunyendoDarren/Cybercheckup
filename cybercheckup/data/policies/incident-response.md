## What counts as an incident
A scam payment, a hacked account, a lost or stolen phone or laptop, a virus or ransomware message, customer data sent to the wrong person, or anything that feels wrong.

## In the first hour
1. **Tell {{owner}} straight away.** Do not hide it and do not try to fix it quietly. Nobody is punished for reporting quickly.
2. **Stop the damage.**
   - Hacked account: change the password from a clean device and sign out all sessions.
   - Stolen phone or SIM: call the telecom to block the SIM, and freeze mobile money.
   - Wrongly sent payment: call the bank or mobile money provider and ask to recall it.
   - Ransomware: unplug the computer from the network and Wi-Fi. Do not pay. Do not switch it off if advised otherwise by your IT helper.
3. **Keep the evidence.** Do not delete messages, emails or files. Take screenshots with the time visible.
4. **Write down what happened:** what, when, which accounts, devices or people were involved.

## Afterwards
- Tell affected customers or staff if their data may be at risk, and check whether you must report to the data protection regulator (in Kenya, the ODPC). Some laws require this within a short time, so check your regulator's current guidance.
- Within a week, hold a short, blame-free review: what happened, what stopped it, what should change?
- Update this plan and the staff briefing.
